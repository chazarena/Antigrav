About Us

Providing expert engineering services across metro and regional Victoria

Our team provides professional engineering services in the core disciplines of Electrical, Fire, Hydraulic, Lifts and Mechanical services, and Environmentally Sustainable Design Consulting (ESD).

We apply Environmental Sustainable Design (ESD) principles to our projects to improve the health and comfort of buildings for occupants, whilst at the same time reducing negative impacts on the environment.

When combined, our design and engineering services:

  • Create efficient, optimised utilities and building systems
  • Protect people and our environment
  • Identify opportunities to save money, energy and resource use
  • Improve living and working conditions
  • Help build happy, healthy communities

Our office provides professional documentation in both AutoCAD and Revit package suites to suit the design team documentation.

Who We Are

ASCOT is driven by senior engineers who each have a wealth of industry experience across disciplines, sectors and technologies. We're a passionate bunch of experts, focused on thorough analysis and sound decision making – for high-quality, innovative and environmentally conscious results.

Our people are our greatest asset and we pride ourselves on the quality of our team.

What We Do

ASCOT is a multi-disciplinary consultancy firm, providing full-scope Building Services Engineering and Environmental Sustainable Design (ESD) services. Every building is unique, so our approach is too.

We tailor our engineering expertise in Mechanical, Electrical, Hydraulics, Fire Safety, Movement & Access and ESD to deliver custom solutions for our clients.

Experience

We have completed projects across Victoria, across education, retail, aged care, community and commercial sectors. Though our projects differ widely in complexity, size, and end-use, we rise to the challenge — optimising every build in safety, quality, and sustainability for the benefit of everyone who lives, plays, studies and works there.
